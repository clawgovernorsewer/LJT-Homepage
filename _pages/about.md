## About

I am Junteng Liu, a first-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) NLP Group. My PhD supervisor is Professor Junxian He, who previously advised me during my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU). I graduated from SJTU in June 2024 with a B.Eng.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. My research interests include LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs), and LLM truthfulness and interpretability.

A structured publication list is available on the [Publications page]({{ base_path }}/publications/), and my complete publication record is available on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=tbK9jl4AAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate).
